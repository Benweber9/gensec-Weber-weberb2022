Sample Cybersecurity Incident Report
=================================

Date: 2026-10-22
Incident ID: SAMPLE-INC-2026-001

Summary:
A phishing email was reported by a staff member in the finance department. The email
claimed to be from a trusted vendor and requested an urgent account verification.
The user did not click any links and reported the message to the security team.

Observed indicators:
- Suspicious sender address using a lookalike domain
- Urgent language asking for immediate action
- Generic greeting instead of a personalized address
- Request to verify credentials outside the normal process

Recommended actions:
- Block the sender and related domains
- Notify the help desk for user awareness follow-up
- Review mail logs and quarantine similar messages
- Update phishing awareness training materials

Status:
Closed after review; no account compromise was confirmed.
